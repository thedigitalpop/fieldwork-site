Most of us spend our days on autopilot — seeing without noticing, hearing without listening, touching without feeling.

Fieldwork is a daily practice. Each challenge draws your attention to one sense, one moment, one detail you would have missed. Over weeks, the world grows richer — not because it changed, but because you did.

How It Works

The Six Senses

Every sense is a doorway

Fieldwork trains six channels of perception. Each has its own mastery track, color, and progression — ten levels to unlock across sixty unique titles.

Sight

Patterns of light, shadow, color, and shape that your eyes skim past every day.

"Notice the exact color of the sky at this moment."

Sound

The layered symphony beneath the noise — rhythms, silences, and frequencies.

"Count how many distinct sounds you can hear right now."

Touch

Texture, temperature, pressure — the conversation between your body and the world.

"Feel three different textures within arm's reach."

Smell

The most primal sense — bypasses thought and speaks directly to memory.

"Identify an unexpected scent in your environment."

Taste

Flavor is attention. Slow down and let a single bite unfold on your tongue.

"Eat one bite of your next meal with complete attention."

Body

Proprioception, balance, internal signals — the sense that tells you where you are.

"Notice how your weight shifts as you stand still."
